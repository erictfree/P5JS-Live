p5js live

AUDIO-REACTIVE VISUAL LIVE CODING IN THE BROWSER

p5js live is a browser-based visual instrument for live-coding audio-reactive graphics with JavaScript and p5.js. It was created by Eric Freeman at the Department of Arts and Entertainment Technologies at the University of Texas at Austin.

Choose what the visuals should respond to:

Choose an audio file (.mp3, .wav, .ogg, .m4a, or .aac), microphone, or silence to begin.
